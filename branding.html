Visual Identity & Branding

A short selection of brand identities I designed, each translated brand's goals into a consistent visual system across logo, colour, typography, and communication materials.

Roles
Brand identity · Logo design · Visual systems · Communication design

Tools
Figma · Adobe Illustrator · Adobe Photoshop

Meko Mesto

Brand identity for a creative craft space for women. The logo is hand-drawn inspired by font Lostar. The brand reflects the handmade, women focused nature of the space. The colour palette combines earthy terracotta, olive, and cream to feel grounded and welcoming.

The full identity includes logo variations, colour palette, typography, and application across merchandise: tote bags, pins, stickers and instagram posts.

Storia

Brand identity for an AI-powered screen-free storytelling toy for children. The visual language is playful and multi-coloured. It is designed to feel child-friendly without looking childish.

The system includes a custom logo, full colour palette, pattern design, presentation templates and logo variations across multiple backgrounds. Designed to work across exhibition materials, communication materials and digital platforms

Horizon

Brand identity for a collaborative AI assistant for local tour operators. The identity needed to feel professional and trustworthy while still being approachable. A compass mark in the wordmark communicates direction and guidance

The full system covers logo variations, colour palette, typography, icon set, and product application across the web platform.
